About PhishGuard

Helping you pause before you click.

PhishGuard is a phishing link detection system built to make checking suspicious URLs straightforward.

Our purpose

Phishing messages can use convincing links to send people to harmful or misleading websites. PhishGuard helps users examine a URL before visiting it, so they can make a more informed decision.

We also encourage a simple habit: take a moment to verify unexpected links, especially when a message asks for account details, payment, or urgent action.
